Welcome to Bryn Barcud Bach

Make yourself at home.

Our cottage is a lovingly converted 17th-century milking parlour in a peaceful rural hamlet above the Conwy Valley. It retains much of its original charm, with everything you need for a comfortable stay. The cottage is all on one level and powered by solar energy. We hope you’ll find it a cosy retreat for two, with countryside walks and wildlife on the doorstep.

Inside, we’ve made sure you’ll find a comfortable living area, a well-equipped kitchen, a double bedroom and a shower room. Everything you need for a relaxed stay.

Fresh air & open space

Life in the countryside.

Enjoy open green space, surrounding hills and the slower pace of the countryside. It's a lovely base for exploring the Conwy Valley and the wider landscape of North Wales.

Whether you're heading out for a walk or simply enjoying a quiet morning, there's plenty to appreciate close to home.

Our cottage is surrounded by working farmland, with cows, sheep and chickens among your neighbours. Look out for the majestic red kites that give the cottage its name, often soaring overhead. You might also spot buzzards circling above the hills or rabbits darting through the grass.

Living & dining

Open-plan living, kitchen and dining space with a sofa, armchair, electric fire and a dining table for two. The well-equipped kitchen has a microwave and washing machine; Bluetooth audio and a DVD player are also provided.

Bedroom & bathroom

A cosy double bedroom with wardrobe, chest of drawers, bedside tables and remote-controlled television. The contemporary bathroom has a walk-in shower with one step up and a heated towel rail.

Outside

Enjoy outdoor dining overlooking open countryside, or take a morning coffee to the paddock seating area and watch red kites soaring overhead. There's even a secure paddock for dog walking.

Included comforts

Bed linen, towels, bathrobes, hairdryer, high-speed Wi-Fi and USB charging are included. Off-road parking is available, and secure bicycle storage if needed.

Explore North Wales

From quiet walks to big adventures

You’ll find us in a small rural hamlet near Trofarth, above the Conwy Valley. Enjoy walks from the door, coast and mountains within reach, or simply stay put and enjoy the wildlife.

Eglwysbach & Bodnant

The village of Eglwysbach is around three miles away, with countryside walks and the Bee Inn. Visit Bodnant Garden, particularly its spring laburnum arch, and the nearby Bodnant Welsh Food centre.

Conwy & the coast

Explore Conwy’s medieval walls and castle, independent shops, cafés, pubs and restaurants. Deganwy, Llandudno, Rhos-on-Sea, Colwyn Bay and Llanrwst offer more places to visit. There are beaches and stretches of the Wales Coast Path to discover.

Eryri National Park

Head into Eryri (Snowdonia) for mountain walks, lakes, climbing and a possible ascent of Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon). Near Betws-y-Coed, mountain bikers can tackle the Gwydir Mawr Trail.

More to do

Try underground adventures at Go Below, ride the world's fastest zip wire at Penrhyn Quarry, or enjoy a gentler day watching the landscape and local birds from the cottage.

Before you arrive

Practical information

Access & suitability

All accommodation is on the ground floor, but the cottage isn't officially wheelchair accessible as the shower has a step up. Designed for two adults, the cottage isn't suitable for children or infants. The property is strictly non-smoking.

Dogs & connectivity

Up to two well-behaved medium-sized dogs are welcome; Please call or message us to discuss any specific pet requirements. High-speed Wi-Fi is provided, but mobile phone reception can be limited depending on the network.

Stays & arrivals

Short breaks are available all year, with a minimum stay of three nights and flexible arrival days. Please check current availability on Independent Cottages.
